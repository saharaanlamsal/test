Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will I get my test registration or booking confirmation?+

Most bookings are confirmed within 30 minutes to a few hours after payment, depending on the exam and test center availability.

Do I need an international card to pay?+

No. You can pay fully in NPR through eSewa or a direct bank transfer from any Nepali bank — no foreign currency card required.

Is the test registration or booking official?+

Yes. Every confirmation comes directly from the official test provider — the same booking you'd get going through the provider's own site.

Can I reschedule or cancel my test after booking?+

Rescheduling and cancellation follow the official test provider's standard policy. Message us and we'll guide you through the process.

Which cities do you cover?+

We can book test centers across Nepal — including Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Butwal — based on where you'd like to sit the exam.
